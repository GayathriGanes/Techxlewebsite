Challenges

The company experienced inefficiencies in managing lab supplies, resulting in frequent stockouts, over-ordering, and delays in research projects due to the unavailability of critical materials.

Solutions

An Inventory Management system was introduced to centralize and automate stock tracking for lab supplies. Features included:

  • Real-time inventory tracking for lab consumables and reagents.
  • Automatic reordering of materials when stock levels were low.
  • Integration with purchasing systems to manage suppliers and track order status.

Impact

The company saw a 40% reduction in stockouts and a 25% decrease in over-ordering. Researchers had access to the supplies they needed, reducing project delays and enabling more accurate financial forecasting for lab expenses.
