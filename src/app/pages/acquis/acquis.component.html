Challenges

The institute faced challenges in managing large equipment acquisitions and maintaining proper documentation for capital expenditures. Manual processes led to delays in procurement, miscommunication between departments, and budget overruns.

Solutions

The institute deployed an Acquisition Support for Labs system, which streamlined the acquisition of lab equipment by automating procurement workflows and ensuring compliance with financial regulations. The system enabled:

  • Automated request submission for new lab equipment.
  • Transparent budget tracking for acquisitions.
  • Workflow integration with the finance department to streamline approvals.

Impact

Acquisition timelines were reduced by 35%, and the system helped the institute stay within budget for equipment procurement. Financial reporting became more accurate, and the administrative workload for procurement teams was reduced by 50%.
