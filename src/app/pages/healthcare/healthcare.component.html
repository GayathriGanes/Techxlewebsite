Challenges

Thrizer’s existing healthcare portal lacked scalability and efficiency, impeding their ability to manage increased user demands and integrate new features. They required a robust solution that could enhance performance and support business growth.

Solutions

We developed a full-stack application using Angular and Node.js. Angular provided a modern, user-friendly interface, while Node.js offered a scalable back-end. This combination ensured that the portal could efficiently handle higher user volumes and adapt to future needs.

Impact

The scalable application led to a 30% increase in sales within six months. Enhanced performance and user capacity enabled Thrizer to significantly boost their sales. This result highlights the effectiveness of a well-designed, scalable solution in fostering business growth.
