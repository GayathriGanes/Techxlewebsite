Challenges

The company had difficulty managing its portfolio of drug development projects across different departments. They lacked visibility into project progress, resource allocation, and potential risks, resulting in duplicated efforts and resource bottlenecks.

Solutions

The company adopted a Project Portfolio Management system that allowed for centralized tracking and management of all drug development projects. Features included:

  • Real-time project dashboards with progress indicators.
  • Resource management tools to allocate teams and lab resources effectively.
  • Risk management features to identify potential project delays early.

Impact

The system increased transparency across departments, reducing project duplication and enabling better resource management Project timelines were shortened by 20%, and the company was able to bring drugs to market faster. Project risk management improved, with the system identifying potential delays 40% earlier than before.
