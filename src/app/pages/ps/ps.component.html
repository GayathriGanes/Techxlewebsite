Challenges

The company faced challenges in tracking employees' daily activities and project progress. Lack of integration with existing tools led to inefficiencies and difficulty in generating meaningful reports.

Solutions

To enhance tracking and reporting, we implemented the following features using the Power Apps platform:

  • Integration with DevOps Tools:Seamless project tracking by connecting with existing DevOps tools.
  • AI-Generated MSRs:Automated Monthly Status Reports (MSRs) generated using AI for improved accuracy and efficiency.
  • Employee Directory: A centralized directory for quick access to employee information.
  • PTO Management: Tools for tracking Paid Time Off (PTO) requests and balances.

Impact

These enhancements are expected to improve visibility into daily activities, streamline project tracking, and facilitate better reporting. This will ultimately lead to increased productivity and more effective resource management across the organization.
